nightfall-display-bold.woff2
============================

The site's title face: a Helvetica Bold match, self-hosted so titles look
the same on Windows (which has no Helvetica and would substitute Arial),
macOS, iOS and Android.

Derived from TeX Gyre Heros Bold, version 2.004 (texgyreheros-bold.otf),
by the GUST e-foundry. Original files:
  https://ctan.org/pkg/tex-gyre-heros
  https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/heros

Changes made: subset to Latin (U+0020–007E, U+00A0–017F), general
punctuation (U+2010–2027, U+2030–203A), €, ™, arrows (U+2190–2199) and
minus (U+2212); converted from OpenType CFF to WOFF2. No outlines edited.
